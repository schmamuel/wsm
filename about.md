Spoons Map is a free, independent tool that lets you find every J.D. Wetherspoon pub across the UK (and Spain) on a single interactive map. Whether you're looking for your nearest local, planning a pub crawl, or just curious how many Spoons are in your city, this is the quickest way to find out.

## What is Spoons Map?

Spoons Map plots every Wetherspoon pub in the United Kingdom on an interactive map, showing opening status, address, and a direct link to the pub's official page. Click any marker to see the details, get directions, or share the pub with a friend.

The site is updated regularly to reflect new openings and closures across the Wetherspoon estate, which currently spans over 800 pubs from Inverness to Penzance.

## How to use it

Using Spoons Map is straightforward. The map loads with all UK Wetherspoon locations marked. You can:

- **Zoom and pan** to explore any area of the UK
- **Click a marker** to see the pub name, address, and opening status
- **Get directions** directly to any pub via Google Maps
- **Share a pub** with friends using the share button in the popup
- **Use your location** to find the nearest Wetherspoon to where you are now

## Why we built it

The official Wetherspoon website is great for finding individual pubs but does not offer a map view of the full estate. Spoons Map fills that gap - giving you a bird's eye view of every pub in the chain at once, useful for travellers, regulars, and the simply curious.

## Independent and free

Spoons Map is completely free to use and is not affiliated with J.D. Wetherspoon plc in any way. It is an independent project built and maintained by a Wetherspoon enthusiast.
