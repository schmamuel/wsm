## Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 2026

Spoons Map is a free, independent tool for finding Wetherspoon pubs. It is not affiliated with J.D. Wetherspoon plc in any way.

## What we collect

We use Google Analytics to understand how people use this site. This collects anonymous data including pages visited, time spent on site, your approximate location, device type, and browser. This data is aggregated and we cannot identify you individually from it.

We do not collect your name, email address, or any other personal information. We do not require you to create an account.

## Cookies

Google Analytics sets cookies on your device to track sessions and distinguish users. These are analytics cookies only. By using this site you consent to these cookies being set.

## Third parties

We use the following third party services:

- **Google Analytics** - usage analytics. [Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
- **OpenStreetMap & CARTO** - map tiles. [CARTO's privacy policy](https://carto.com/privacy)
- **Leaflet** - open source map library, no data collected

We do not sell or share your data with any other third parties.

## Your rights

If you are based in the UK or EU you have the right to access, correct, or request deletion of any personal data held about you. As we hold no personal data beyond anonymous analytics this is unlikely to be relevant, but you can contact us if you have any questions.

You can opt out of Google Analytics tracking at any time using the [Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on](https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout).
